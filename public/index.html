🌐 Open Source

Why Open Innovation Matters

🔒

Your memories stay yours

Every word you type, every story you share — it runs on your machine via Ollama. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or monetised. Open-source AI makes that guarantee possible.

🌑

Works in the dark

No internet? No problem. Spiritus is fully offline. A closed API would need a server and your data to cross the wire. An open-weight model just needs your laptop.

🔧

Swap, fine-tune, own it

Don't like Gemma? Pull a Mistral or a LLaMA. Fine-tune on your family's letters. Open weights mean you control the model — not the other way around.

♾️

It won't disappear

Closed AI services shut down. A local model lives as long as you keep the file. Spiritus will still work in 20 years because the weights are yours to keep.
